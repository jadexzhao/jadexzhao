Who it is for

Someone who needs to open Yelp for Business, check the listing, update hours or photos, and reply to reviews.

Yelp for Business (biz.yelp.com or the Yelp for Business app) is the owner desk. Handoff means you can find that desk, keep the page accurate, and respond to a review without the builder sitting there. Do not write login passwords on this page.

Steps

  1. Open biz.yelp.com (or the Yelp for Business app) and sign in with the owner account.
  2. Check you are on the right location. If more than one business shows, pick yours from the location list.
  3. To update hours, address, phone, or photos, open the business info or page settings area and edit what changed. Save when you finish.
  4. To read reviews, open Reviews in the left menu (or More on the app). Scroll to the review you want.
  5. To reply in public, click or tap Respond under that review, type a short reply, then send. Use Direct message only when you need a private follow-up.
  6. If the page is not claimed yet, start at biz.yelp.com/claim, search the business name, and follow the claim and verification steps. Someone with owner access has to finish verification.

Menu labels move a little when Yelp redesigns the desk. Stay in the same places: the location picker, business info, Reviews, and Respond.
