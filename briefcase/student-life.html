IU Student LifeMy S.U.C.C.E.E.D. footprint · curated, not a certification

HHSP uses the S.U.C.C.E.E.D. framework so scholars can name growth across Scholarship, Uplift, Community, Creativity, Empowerment, Engagement, and Development. Programme levels (cream, crimson, golden, platinum) are milestones on a shared rubric, not a rank on my CV. Below is where I have actually shown up; hire proof still leads on home and Serve IT.

Scholarship Uplift Community Creativity Empowerment Engagement Development

Different paths, same campus

No two Hudson & Holland scholars stack these pillars the same way, and that is fine. Cream, crimson, golden, and platinum are guideposts in the programme, not a competition between classmates. My path leans clinic ships, FASE MAP coordination, and Digital Humans research; yours might lean lab hours, ensemble nights, athletics, or advocacy I have never tried. If our calendars look nothing alike, that is alright. What matters is showing up honestly and leaving something useful behind, especially when you are the first in your family to learn the campus map.
