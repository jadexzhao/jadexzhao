Student life

HHSP Program Assistant work with the Office of Student Life. First-generation student. Campus work that asks me to show up, make the room more navigable, and help someone else keep their agency in it.

Thank you to the people who staff HHSP events.

A first-generation path is not a brand story with the hard parts removed. It is rooms, people, setup, questions, and the small work that helps someone else see a way through.

Program Assistant

I'm a first-generation student. As a Program Assistant for HHSP I help at campus events and with the quieter setup that makes those hours run ... chairs, check-in, the odd job nobody put on a flyer. Those details are not background; they decide whether a room feels possible to enter.
