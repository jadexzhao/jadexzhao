This page is for a small business or restaurant person who will upload videos after the builder leaves.

Who Steps Video

Who it is for

Someone who needs to open the business YouTube channel, upload a video, and publish or schedule it.

YouTube Studio is the desk where channel owners manage videos. Handoff means you can find Studio, upload a file, fill in the basics, and save or publish without the builder sitting there. Do not write login passwords on this page.

Same stuck moment that turns into a support ticket. Short steps and a video so you do not have to ping the builder.

Steps

  1. Open studio.youtube.com and sign in with the Google account that owns (or can manage) the channel.
  2. Check the channel name at the top. If more than one channel is linked, switch to the business channel you need.
  3. Click Create, then Upload videos. Choose the video file from your computer.
  4. Add a clear title and a short description. Pick a thumbnail if you have one. Answer the audience question (made for kids or not).
  5. Click Next through the checks screens. On Visibility, choose Public, Unlisted, Private, or Schedule a date and time. Then click Publish, Save, or Schedule.
  6. To find an old video later, open Content in the left menu. Search by title, or scroll the list.

Button names move a little when YouTube redesigns Studio. Stay in the same places: Create, Upload, Content, and Visibility.
