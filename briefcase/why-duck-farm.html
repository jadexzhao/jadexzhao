Why a duck farm

The product is whether they can keep using it ... even when the product is mud, gates, and morning chores. A duck farm is a long want, not a place I have opened.

Family restaurant, central Indiana Friday night was the floor, the till, and the kitchen, all at once, in a family Chinese restaurant in central Indiana (Greenfield, Indiana). A system either survives contact with a real customer or it does not; I still trust that test more than a plan that only works when the room is quiet. Serve IT is the same instinct with a partner in the room: staff keep the site, not students.
Clinic handoff The work I respect is unglamorous and has to hold for the people who depend on it. At the Laurie Burns McRobbie Serve IT Clinic, a semester is not finished if partners cannot run it after we leave. If they cannot keep using it, the build did not ship.
Why a duck farm Animals, water, feed, a gate that has to close, mornings that do not care whether the idea was elegant. Gardening and long walks pull me outside; the farm is the long version, not a hobby turned into a company. There is a small sketch on this site; I have not opened a farm. I want to show up the way a restaurant has to open, and leave a place other people can keep.
