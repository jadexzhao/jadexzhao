Why a duck farm

I want a duck farm the way I want a system that has to open in the morning whether or not the idea was elegant. Mud, gates, and chores. A long want. Not a farm I have opened.

01 · Origin

Family restaurant

Central Indiana Friday night was the floor, the till, and the kitchen, all at once, in a family Chinese restaurant in central Indiana (Greenfield, Indiana). A system either survives contact with a real customer or it does not. I still trust that test more than a plan that only works when the room is quiet.

02 · The want

Animals, water, gates

A long want Animals, water, feed, a gate that has to close, mornings that do not care whether the idea was elegant. Gardening and long walks pull me outside. The farm is the long version of that, not a hobby turned into a company. I want to show up the way a restaurant has to open, and leave a place other people can keep.

03 · The sketch

A pond on this site

Self-tested There is a small pond sketch here that I poke at myself. I have not opened a farm. The sketch is not a launch. The product is whether it can hold the next morning.
