01 · Role

Project lead

Digital Humans · Luddy Undergraduate project lead on how AI-driven virtual personas can become useful business interfaces when nothing flashy is happening. Informatics at Indiana University Bloomington (IU) Luddy, with UX (user experience) Research and Business Technology minors; student researcher voice, not a product launch claim. Complements shipped clinic work on Serve IT. When a system looks and communicates like a person, the product is still whether they can keep using it.

02 · Scope

Who, what, how judged

Who it is for: organisations weighing AI-driven customer and employee interfaces. What I work on: trust, disclosure, adoption, and human oversight at ordinary touchpoints. How judged: useful after the demo, not impressive in the demo.

03 · Focus

Focus areas

  • Research questions Human-computer interaction (HCI) and human-centred AI: what makes people want to use, trust, and keep using an interface that behaves like a person. High-level undergraduate research at Luddy; not a shipped commercial product claim on this page. Disclosure and expectations have to match what the system actually does.
  • Builder continuity Serve IT handoffs, accessibility audits, and client web work under Jade🪴 test the same bar in municipal, nonprofit, and small-business rooms.
