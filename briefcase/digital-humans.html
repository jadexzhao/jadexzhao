Digital Humans

I'm an Informatics student at Luddy and the student project lead for Digital Humans. The question is product-sized: when AI looks and communicates like a person, what earns trust? I am building the research before I claim the answer.

Thank you to the people who let me keep asking this question. I still have more homework than answers.

The work

When a system looks and talks like a person, I want to know what makes someone willing to use it, and what it should tell them first.

I look at disclosure, whether people keep using it, and who is responsible when something goes wrong.

In HCI (human-computer interaction), I want that disclosure to match what the system actually does. That's the part I keep coming back to.
