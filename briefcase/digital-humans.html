Digital Humans

I am an Informatics student at Luddy, and Digital Humans is the work I am most invested in here: human-centred AI that has to work as a business interface, not a demo reel. When a system looks and communicates like a person, trust, disclosure, and adoption still get decided on an ordinary Tuesday.

Why this project

Why Informatics at Luddy is the right home

Informatics, for me, is people, organisations, and technology in one loop: who the system is for, whether they can use it, and whether anyone will keep using it after the launch video ends. Digital Humans is where that loop gets sharp ... when the interface anthropomorphises itself and users still have to make real decisions about trust.

I did not pick this major to collect buzzwords. I picked it because I like the unglamorous questions: what should be disclosed, what do users expect, who stays accountable, and what makes someone come back next week. Luddy lets me pursue those as an undergraduate project lead with UX (user experience) Research and Business Technology minors, and Serve IT keeps the same bar honest in partner rooms.

02 · Scope

Who, what, how judged

Who it is for: organisations weighing AI-driven customer and employee interfaces. What I work on: trust, disclosure, adoption, and human oversight at ordinary touchpoints. How judged: useful after the demo, not impressive in the demo.

03 · Focus

Focus areas

  • Research questions Human-computer interaction (HCI) and human-centred AI: what makes people want to use, trust, and keep using an interface that behaves like a person. 04 · OVPUE

Undergraduate research with IU Undergraduate Education

Office of the Vice Provost for Undergraduate Education (OVPUE) · UX (user experience) Aug to Dec 2025: undergraduate research on privacy and consumer technology with IU Undergraduate Education, centred on whether people can understand what they are consenting to in everyday products. This was office-sponsored undergraduate research and creative inquiry under Undergraduate Education, not an NSF Research Experiences for Undergraduates (REU) placement. IU Undergraduate Research (IUUR) sits in the same OVPUE unit and describes its work as supporting research and creative inquiry for interested students across campus. Same thread as Digital Humans: disclosure, plain language, and consent UX before any trust claim ... high level on this page, no clinical or private-lab framing.

This is the Luddy lane I care about most, so I try to match the enthusiasm with discipline. (Ethics coursework at Luddy is part of how I approach research methods here: informed participation, plain disclosure, consent framing before claims, and refusing to overstate what undergraduate HCI work can support in public.)
