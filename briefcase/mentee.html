Mentee conversations

I am a mentee as well as someone who mentors. People who work at MAANG (Meta, Amazon, Apple, Netflix, Google), Big Three strategy firms, Big Four professional services firms, and Fortune 100 companies have given me career and craft advice. That is input I test on real work. It is not a job title and not a claim that I worked at those organisations.

No named mentors, no private conversation detail, and no invented internships.

What I ask for

The useful questions are ordinary. How do you scope a first version. How do you explain a trade-off to someone who will not read the repo. What does a consultant or software engineer actually own after the slide deck. What would you cut if this had to ship on Tuesday.

Big Three here means strategy consulting (McKinsey, Boston Consulting Group, and Bain as a category). Big Four means professional services (Deloitte, PwC, EY, and KPMG as a category). Fortune 100 means large public companies. I do not list individual firms as if each one formally sponsored me.

What I do with the advice

I take the advice back to Digital Humans work, ServeIT clinic builds, family restaurant operations, and student software projects. If it does not survive a real customer, a partner handoff, or a person who needs an honest answer, I do not keep it as a slogan.

This is separate from FASE MAP (Faculty and Staff for Student Excellence Mentor Apprenticeship Programme), where I am a peer mentor for first-generation and low-income students.
