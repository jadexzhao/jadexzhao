Independent · family restaurant

Potstickers

New Palestine, Indiana. Tuesday to Sunday, 11 AM to 9 PM. Closed Mondays.

01 / Context

The problem

A family restaurant has to take orders and tell people the hours every service. During the pandemic I started building the digital side so online ordering was not a rumour. The bar is still the same: it works on Tuesday, or it does not.

02 / My role

What I did

Digital and operations, August 2019 to August 2023 on the résumé, and the site is still mine to keep. Website, online ordering, POS, digital menus, and online marketing.

03 / Requirements

Constraints

Staff have to update hours and dishes without a developer on call. Guests need a menu and a place to order. Fancy frameworks that only I can deploy are a liability.

04 / Implementation

Approach

Customer HTML in the pstickers24 repo, embedded on a Google Site. Clover for online orders. Menu photos, alt text, and posting guides live in that repository. Formspree is on the contact path.

05 / Evidence

What you can open

  • Google Site
  • Order online (Clover)
  • Repository
06 / Outcomes

What is true

The restaurant still runs Tuesday to Sunday. The site, order link, and repo exist. I am not adding revenue numbers I do not have.

07 / Reflection

What I learned

This is where software stopped being homework. If a page is wrong, someone does not eat, or someone calls the dining room. I still use that as a test on clinic work.
