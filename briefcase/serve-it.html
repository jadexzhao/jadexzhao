Serve IT

Partner semestersOne ladder, four handoffs

I joined Serve IT as a freshman. That semester, Jan 2024 to May 2024, the clinic took 19 students from 125 applications, about a 6.5% acceptance rate, and I started as an accessibility tester. That rate is only for the semester I got in.

The tester semester is the start of the ladder. WCAG (Web Content Accessibility Guidelines) 2.1, keyboard and screen reader, then written recommendations. It is not a client case of its own, and I am not a cybersecurity or Tableau student on the other clinic teams. The four sections below are the partners.

The capstone path is INFO I389, then INFO I489: 6 credits over two semesters, and a minimum of 3 semesters in the clinic. I489 needs those 3 credits of I389 already done here, good standing, the clinic application, and the core courses finished. INFO I300 or I308 can still be open, but only alongside the first three credits of I489. My semesters have been website work and accessibility work. The clinic also has cybersecurity, data visualisation, and Teach IT teams. Serve AI is a separate project. I have not done that work.

Aug 2024 to Dec 2024

Middle Way House

Middle Way House is a Bloomington nonprofit that supports people affected by domestic violence, sexual assault, and human trafficking. On a site like that, essential information is not decoration. Someone may be looking for help.

I was the WordPress redesign lead, with staff and a student team. The semester asked us to rebuild the journeys so that information is findable, and to do it with the people who would still be there in January.

What I handed back was a site structured for that findability, built so staff could keep it without a student team on retainer. The limit is the handoff. I did not stay as their developer.

Jan 2025 to May 2025

Indiana Phenology

Indiana Phenology is a nonprofit for citizen science on seasonal change in nature. The public is invited to observe. Researchers, educators, and residents also need the records.

I was the web development lead. The semester asked for navigation that could hold two kinds of visit at once: someone coming from outreach, and someone coming for the research. Accessibility sat in that same structure, not in a separate appendix.

What I handed back was a way through the site for those visitors, made so the organisation could keep it after the team left.

Aug 2025 to Dec 2025

Greater Bloomington Chamber of Commerce

The Greater Bloomington Chamber of Commerce supports local businesses in Bloomington. Many of those businesses do not have a developer on call. The chamber site has to be something staff can explain and maintain.

I was the web designer, working with staff. Stakeholder asks had to become page-level decisions: navigation, structure, and accessibility. A request in a meeting was not finished until it had a place on a page.

What I handed back was those pages, decided with staff, so they could keep them. I did not leave them a student on retainer.

Aug 2026 to present

Town of Nashville

The Town of Nashville website is the municipal site for Nashville, Brown County, Indiana. Residents use it for council, utilities, police, and other local information.

I am the accessibility auditor. The semester asks for an assessment of townofnashville.org against WCAG 2.1: keyboard paths and screen-reader paths, then a prioritised list the town can act on after the clinic leaves.

What comes back is recommendations. Students do not edit the live site. This is not a rebuild. The in-progress rubric page for the semester is the Nashville case study.
