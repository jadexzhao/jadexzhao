The first semester, Jan 2024 to May 2024, was my first year as an intern, with the City of Bloomington. I was an accessibility tester at the Laurie Burns McRobbie Serve IT Clinic, the bottom of the ladder. WCAG (Web Content Accessibility Guidelines) 2.1 as a habit: keyboard and screen reader, then written recommendations. That start is not a second Nashville. I am not a cybersecurity or Tableau student on the other clinic teams. The five sections below are chronological: Bloomington first, then the longer partner write-ups.

The capstone path is INFO I389, then INFO I489: 6 credits over two consecutive semesters, and a minimum of 3 semesters in the clinic. I489 needs those 3 credits of I389 already done here, good standing, the clinic application, and the core courses finished. INFO I300 or I308 can still be open, but only alongside the first three credits of I489. My semesters have been website work and accessibility work. The clinic also has cybersecurity, data visualisation, and Teach IT teams. Serve AI is a separate project. I have not done that work.

How I run a semester: empathise, define the problem, build, hand off. If partners cannot keep using it after we leave, the semester did not finish.

Jan 2024 to May 2024

First year as an intern. Accessibility tester, City of Bloomington

The City of Bloomington was my first semester at the Laurie Burns McRobbie Serve IT Clinic: accessibility tester, not a site rebuild and not a second Nashville.

Who it was for: residents using city digital services, including people on keyboard and screen-reader paths. What shipped: WCAG (Web Content Accessibility Guidelines) 2.1 testing as a habit, keyboard and screen reader runs, written recommendations. How judged: clear findings the city could act on; the bottom of the ladder I still climb from.

Aug 2024 to Dec 2024

Middle Way House

Middle Way House is a Bloomington nonprofit that supports people affected by domestic violence, sexual assault, and human trafficking. The person I kept in mind was someone looking for help, and the staff member who would still be there in January.

Essential information was too hard to find. On a site like that, a buried page is not a style problem. I was the WordPress redesign lead, with staff and a student team. We rebuilt the journeys so that information is findable, and we did it with the people who would keep the site.

What I handed back was that structure, built so staff could run it without a student team on retainer. The limit is the handoff. I did not stay as their developer. What carried forward: if they cannot keep it, the semester did not finish.

Jan 2025 to May 2025

Indiana Phenology

Indiana Phenology is a nonprofit for citizen science on seasonal change in nature. Two people use it: someone arriving from outreach, and someone who needs the research records. Educators and residents sit in both groups.

One path could not hold both visits. I was the web development lead. We built navigation for those two arrivals at once, and put accessibility in that structure rather than in a separate appendix.

What I handed back was a way through the site, made so the organisation could keep it after the team left. What carried forward: a meeting about "the public" is not done until you can name which public, and give each one a path.

Aug 2025 to Dec 2025

Greater Bloomington Chamber of Commerce

The Greater Bloomington Chamber of Commerce supports local businesses in Bloomington. The people who depend on the site are those businesses, many without a developer on call, and the staff who have to explain the pages.

A request in a meeting was not yet a page. I was the web designer, working with staff. Stakeholder asks became decisions about navigation, structure, and accessibility. A request was finished when it had a place someone could find.

What I handed back was those pages, decided with staff, so they could keep them. I did not leave them a student on retainer. What carried forward: the handoff is part of the design, not a favour at the end.

Aug 2026 to present

First year as team lead. Accessibility auditor, Town of Nashville

The Town of Nashville site is the municipal site for Nashville, Brown County, Indiana: council, utilities, police, and town services. Residents depend on it for that local information, including someone using a keyboard or a screen reader.

Who it is for
A resident who uses the civic site, including someone on a keyboard or a screen reader.
In scope
An assessment of townofnashville.org. Keyboard and screen-reader paths, then a prioritised list the town can act on after the clinic leaves.
Out of scope
Live-site edits. The student team does not change the live site. This is not a rebuild.
Delivered
Recommendations only. The longer working page for the semester is the Nashville case study.
Judged by
Whether the paths were tested, and whether the town has a list it can use after the clinic leaves.
