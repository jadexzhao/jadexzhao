Serve IT

Serve IT clinic work for nonprofit partners, Jan 2024 to present. Students build or audit. Staff keep the site after we leave. Every project is a test of whether the next person can run what we made and still make decisions of their own.

Aug 2024 to Dec 2024

Middle Way House

Per their public site, Middle Way House empowers survivors of domestic violence, sexual assault, and human trafficking through support, education, and prevention (middlewayhouse.org).

People use this site when they need Middle Way House. After we leave, staff still have to keep it.

I led a WordPress redesign with the clinic team. Goal: important pages easier to find. About 84 volunteer hours.

Case study · middlewayhouse.org

Jan 2025 to May 2025

Indiana Phenology

Volunteer-run 501(c)(3) founded in 2019. Educators, residents, and 50+ citizen scientists use the site around seasonal change.

I led the web work from Jan 2025 to May 2025. Goal: seasonal-change content easier to find for educators and residents.

Case study · indianaphenology.org

Aug 2025 to Dec 2025

Their public site presents the Chamber as supporting local businesses in Bloomington, Indiana (chamberbloomington.org).
