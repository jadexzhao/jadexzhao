Serve IT

Middle Way House

Per their public site, Middle Way House empowers survivors of domestic violence, sexual assault, and human trafficking through support, education, and prevention (middlewayhouse.org). The website can be a quiet first door. After we leave, staff still have to keep it. About 84 volunteer hours.

  • Migrated and structured content delivery layouts to streamline critical user journeys for individuals seeking crisis services.
  • Implemented clean, maintainable backend schemas and simple system protocols so non-technical staff can manage resources independently.

Case study · middlewayhouse.org
