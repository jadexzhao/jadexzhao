Who it is for

Someone who needs to open Canva, pick a template, change the words and pictures, then download or share the file.

Handoff means you can finish a simple design without the builder sitting there. Do not write the password on this page.

Steps

  1. Open canva.com and sign in with the account that owns the designs.
  2. Click Create a design. Search for Instagram post or Flyer, then open a size that fits what you need.
  3. Pick a template from the side, or start from the blank page. Click text to edit words. Click a picture to replace it.
  4. When it looks right, click Share (top right), then Download. PNG is fine for posts. PDF is fine for a printable flyer.
  5. If you need to send the design to someone, use Share and invite them, or download the file and send the download.

Menus move a little. Stay in the same places: Create a design, Templates, text and images on the page, then Share / Download.
