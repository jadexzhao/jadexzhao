Who Steps Video

Who it is for

Someone who needs to read the inbox, reply, send a new message, and find an old thread.

Handoff means you can do those four things without the builder sitting there. Do not write the password on this page.

Same stuck moment that turns into a support ticket. Short steps and a video so you do not have to ping the builder.

Steps

  1. Open mail.google.com (or the Gmail app) and sign in with the work email.
  2. Look at the Inbox list. Bold lines are usually unread. Click or tap a message to open it.
  3. To answer, click or tap Reply, type, then Send. That keeps the same thread.
  4. To start a new message, click or tap Compose (or the pencil). Fill To, Subject, and the body, then Send.
  5. To find a thread later, use the search box at the top. Type a name, a subject word, or part of the message, then open the result.

Button names move a little between the web and the app. Stay in the same places: Inbox, Reply, Compose, and Search.
