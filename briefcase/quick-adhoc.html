what this is

Quick Ad Hoc is the meeting type title in Outlook / Microsoft Bookings. Public booking page, Teams meeting, 30 minutes. Description on the booking side: “Book time to connect with me.”

Use Book Quick Ad Hoc above for the public Bookings page (Teams, 30 minutes). Email is the fallback if booking is unavailable. Signature links point here first; this page holds the Bookings CTA.

good for

  • quick Faculty and Staff for Student Excellence (FASE) / MAP clarifications for first-generation and low-income students
  • short resume or site questions
  • “can you look at this for 10 min”
  • intro connects that do not need a long agenda

not for

  • emergencies or anything that needs same-hour crisis response
  • clinical or CAPS crisis support ... use campus wellbeing and counseling resources for that
  • long project scoping, multi-stakeholder planning, or anything that needs a real working session

how to reach

  • Preferred: book Quick Ad Hoc via the Book button above (public Microsoft Bookings page).
  • Fallback: jlzhao@iu.edu (primary IU). Optional Outlook if you already have that address. Subject suggestion: Quick ad hoc: [topic]
