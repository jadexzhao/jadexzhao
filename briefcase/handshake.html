04 · Topics

What the posts cover

  • Workplace norms How a workplace actually expects you to show up. Written for students, not as programme-office copy. A norm only helps if someone can follow it on Monday.
  • Professional communication How to say the thing clearly enough that the other person can act. The same bar as a clinic handoff: the reader has to keep going without you in the room. Honest asks beat polished vagueness.
  • Career decisions Early choices about roles, proof, and what to do next. Complements shipped clinic work. It is not a substitute for it. Advice counts when a student can use it the next week.
