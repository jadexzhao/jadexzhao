Town of Nashville ACCAccessibility assessment · recommendations only

This is the work in front of me this term. With Aanya Arora I co-lead ServeIT’s Town of Nashville ACC: look at townofnashville.org, write what staff can actually fix, and hand it over clean. We do not touch the live municipal site.

Municipal sites are public infrastructure. Accessibility is not a polish pass. It is whether a resident can use the page on an ordinary day.

That question sits under the longer Digital Humans work too: useful interfaces people trust when nothing flashy is happening. Nashville is the clear ticket first.

  • Scope: recommendations only. No student edits on the live site.
  • Client: Town of Nashville (Brown County, Indiana).
  • Status: in progress. Mid-semester honesty stays.
