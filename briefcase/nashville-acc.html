Project overview

Problem statement

Residents and visitors need town information and services online. Accessibility barriers on a municipal website shut people out of content they have a right to use. This case study tracks an ACC (accessibility assessment) of townofnashville.org: recommendations the town can act on, not a student redesign of the live site.
