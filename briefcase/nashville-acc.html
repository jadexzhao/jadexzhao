Town of Nashville ACCServeIT Fall 2026 · accessibility assessment

status in progress role Team Lead / Co-lead scope recommendations only

I co-lead ServeIT’s Fall 2026 Town of Nashville ACC: assess accessibility on townofnashville.org, then hand the town clear recommendations they can act on. Recommendations only. No live-site edits. Co-lead with Aanya Arora. I also hold ServeIT Website Team Lead across the clinic.

Elevator pitch: Municipal websites are public infrastructure. When people with disabilities cannot use them, the town loses reach and trust. We evaluate the Town of Nashville site against accessibility practice (including WCAG-minded checks) and package findings so staff can act without a student team on call.

Why this matters in numbers: In WebAIM’s 2025 evaluation of the top 1,000,000 home pages, 94.8% had detectable WCAG 2 failures (average 51 errors per page). (Source: WebAIM Million 2025) In Canada’s 2022 Survey on Disability, 17% of persons with disabilities experienced barriers when using the internet for common online activities. (Source: Statistics Canada)

Problem and role

problem
Public visitors need town information and services online. Accessibility barriers shut people out of government content they have a right to use. The clinic asks for assessment and recommendations, not a redesign sprint on the live domain.

03 · process / approach

How we work (scaffolding)

Process writeup will grow as the assessment runs. Expected shape for a ServeIT ACC (accessibility assessment):

  1. Orient ... team kickoff, scope, tools, and recommendation-only boundary.
  2. Inventory ... map key visitor paths on the town site (information, services, contact).
  3. Evaluate ... accessibility checks (automated where useful, plus human review of critical paths).
  4. Prioritise ... severity, effort, and what staff can maintain without a developer on call.
  5. Recommend ... clear findings and next steps for the town, not unsolicited live edits.
