What this is

Handoff means the next person can open the tool, do the day-to-day job, and find help without the builder sitting next to them. That is the standard: enough control to work, enough clarity to choose.

Support tickets pile up when the build is finished and nobody left a short path with proof. Each guide here is that path: ELI5 steps, a PDF of the same steps, official help links, and a beginner video.

Site builders, CapCut, POS, and DoorDash handoffs live on the classroom set: matchaxmoxie handoffs.
