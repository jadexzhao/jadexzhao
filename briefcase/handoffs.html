Why support tickets pile up

Support tickets spike when the build is finished and nobody showed staff the path.

People open tickets for the same five tasks over and over because nobody left a short path with proof.

These handoffs try to leave that path: ELI5 steps plus a video, so the next person can try it without pinging me.

What this is

Handoff means you can open the tool, do the day-to-day job, and find what you need without the builder sitting next to you.

Everyday tools in learn-first order: email, Apple Notes, Google Drive, Google Reviews / Google Business Profile, Meta / Facebook / Instagram, Canva, LinkedIn, Google Calendar, Yelp, YouTube, and TikTok.

Site builders, CapCut, POS, and DoorDash handoffs live on the classroom set: matchaxmoxie handoffs.
