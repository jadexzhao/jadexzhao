03 · 5 pts

Client description

Client: Indiana Phenology. Agency website: https://www.indianaphenology.org/. Educators, residents, and citizen scientists use the public site around seasonal change and phenology data.

About the agency

Indiana Phenology is a volunteer-run 501(c)(3) founded in 2019. It partners with communities, schools, and individuals. More than 50 citizen scientists provide data. Data visualisation and data questions are part of their work. The public site runs on Wix; the client seems comfortable with data and Wix.

Mission: to increase science-based environmental stewardship by empowering Hoosiers of all ages to participate in citizen science phenology data collection.

Phenology is the timing of seasonal changes in plants and animals. It is a leading indicator of climate impacts. A plain explanation lives on their Learn page.

Educators, residents, and citizen scientists use the site for seasonal change. Our goal was to make those pages easier to find.

My personal Serve IT role on this client was Jan 2025 to May 2025 web work (see overview above).
