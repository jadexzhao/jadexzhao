Who it is for

Someone who needs to open Google Drive, find a file, share a link, and see who already has access.

Handoff means you can do those jobs without the builder sitting there. Do not write the password on this page.

Same stuck moment that turns into a support ticket. Short steps and a video so you do not have to ping the builder.

Steps

  1. Open drive.google.com and sign in with the same Google account that owns the files.
  2. To find a file, use the search box at the top. Type a name or a word from the file, then open the result.
  3. To share, right-click the file (or select it and click Share). Add an email, or choose Copy link if you need a link.
  4. Check the role: Viewer, Commenter, or Editor. Viewer is enough for most handoffs.
  5. Open Share again later to see who has access. Remove someone if they should not keep the file.

Button names move a little. Stay in the same places: Search, Share, the access list, and Copy link.
