02 / Featured engineering

Implemented

BM25 notes search

How do you find the right information in a folder full of notes?

I wrote a Python keyword search that walks a folder of markdown, tokenises it, and ranks files with Okapi BM25. No API key and no vector database. Two files do the ranking: bm25.py and search.py.

A word that is rare across the folder but common in one note helps that note rise. Repeating the word helps less and less. A long note does not automatically win.

03 / AI research

Research · not a launch

Digital Humans

What makes an AI interface useful, understandable, and trustworthy?

Student project lead at Luddy. Digital Humans are interfaces that borrow the social cues of a person. That can make a system easier to approach and easier to misunderstand.

The public question I will stand behind: when an AI system looks and communicates like a person, what actually makes people want to use it, trust it, and keep using it?
