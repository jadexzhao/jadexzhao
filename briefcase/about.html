About

Jade ZhaoInformatics · Serve IT · Digital Humans

The product is whether they can keep using it. I ship accessible web work and audits for partners who cannot keep a developer on call.

Informatics student at the Luddy School, Indiana University Bloomington, with minors in Business Technology and UX (user experience) Research. Digital Humans Project Lead. Serve IT team lead this semester on the Town of Nashville accessibility audit (recommendations only).

Informatics at Luddy is how people, information, and technology fit in one system: who uses it, whether they can use it, whether the organisation can keep it.

I grew up in a family Chinese restaurant in central Indiana (Greenfield, Indiana). Floor, till, kitchen. Friday night. A system survives contact with a real customer or it does not. Serve IT is the same test with a partner instead of a dining room.

The Laurie Burns McRobbie Serve IT Clinic is the Informatics capstone path: INFO I389, then INFO I489, 6 credits over two semesters, minimum 3 semesters in the clinic. I started Jan 2024 to May 2024 as a freshman-year accessibility tester for the City of Bloomington at the clinic (WCAG 2.1, keyboard and screen reader, written recommendations) and I am still in the clinic. The full ladder is on the home page; partner write-ups on case studies.

I lead the Digital Humans project at Luddy: product research on human-centred AI as a business interface, same keep-using-it bar as the clinic. Jade🪴 is client web and branding work I ship under my own name.
