About

Jade Zhaojadexzhao · she/her

I care about accessibility, maintainable systems, and building technology for the people who depend on it. Someday I want to open a duck farm.

I am an Informatics student at the Luddy School, Indiana University Bloomington, with minors in Business Technology and UX (user experience) Research. Informatics, in the way Luddy uses it, is how people, information, and technology fit together. Not just coding, and not just computers: who uses a system, whether they can actually use it, and whether the organisation can keep it.

I grew up in a family Chinese restaurant in central Indiana (Greenfield, Indiana). Floor, till, kitchen. Friday night. A system either survives contact with a real customer or it does not.

Serve IT is the same test, with a partner instead of a dining room. The Laurie Burns McRobbie Serve IT Clinic is the Informatics capstone path: INFO I389, then INFO I489, 6 credits over two semesters, and a minimum of 3 semesters in the clinic. I started as an accessibility tester in Jan 2024 and I am still in it, now auditing the Town of Nashville site. Recommendations only. The full ladder is on the home page.

Digital Humans Project Lead at Luddy. The product is whether they can keep using it: use it, trust it, and still use it after you are gone.
