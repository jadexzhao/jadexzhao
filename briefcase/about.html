About

Jade Zhao

Informatics · Indiana University · expected May 2027

I started building websites during the pandemic so my family's restaurant could take orders online. That is still the lesson: software either survives a real customer, or it does not.

At IU's Laurie Burns McRobbie Serve IT Clinic I grew from a web development, AI and accessibility intern into Technical Project Lead. The work is websites, accessibility checks, documentation, and handoffs that staff can keep running when the student team leaves.

I am a first-generation college student. That is part of why I publish coding notes and handoff guides on matchaxmoxie for first-generation and low-income students finding their way into Informatics.

Off the clock I drink matcha, keep plants, take pictures, walk, and read. A duck farm is a long-term creative throughline, not a product I am selling. Those habits are why this site looks like a garden journal. The case studies are why a hiring manager should stay.

I am interested in forward deployed software engineering because I like ambiguous problems next to real users. I have not held that title. Evidence lives in Serve IT, Potstickers, and the pages under Work.
