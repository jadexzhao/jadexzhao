Thinking in public

Short notes on the questions behind Digital Humans and clinic handoffs. Depth without duplicating whole essays elsewhere.

Core question

When an AI system looks and communicates like a person, what makes someone want to use it, trust it, and keep using it after the first demo?

Plain disclosure

Disclosure has to match what the system actually does. Fancy anthropomorphism without honest limits is how you lose the Monday-after user.

After the demo

Serve IT taught me the same lesson as a family restaurant floor in central Indiana: the system survives contact with a real person or it does not. Research and ships share one bar.
