The clinic

Laurie Burns McRobbie Serve IT Clinic

The Laurie Burns McRobbie Serve IT Clinic is an undergraduate tech clinic and an Informatics capstone at the Luddy School. Course credit, a real nonprofit or town, then a handoff staff can keep. The path is INFO I389, then INFO I489: 6 credits over two consecutive semesters, and a minimum of 3 semesters in the clinic. The partner write-ups follow who the site is for, what they could not do, what we tried, and what we handed back. Those cases are on the case studies page.

I joined Serve IT as a freshman. That semester, Jan 2024 to May 2024, the clinic took 19 students from 125 applications, about a 6.5% acceptance rate, and I started as an accessibility tester. That rate is only for the semester I got in. Each semester after that was a different partner. The list below is one ladder, not five jobs. The partner write-ups are on the case studies page.

  1. Jan 2024 to May 2024

    Accessibility tester, City of Bloomington

    City of Bloomington, at the Laurie Burns McRobbie Serve IT Clinic. The bottom of the ladder. WCAG 2.1 as a habit: keyboard and screen reader, then written recommendations. This is not a second Nashville.

  2. Aug 2024 to Dec 2024

    WordPress redesign lead, Middle Way House

    Middle Way House is a Bloomington nonprofit that supports people affected by domestic violence, sexual assault, and human trafficking. Rebuild with staff and a student team. Restructured journeys so essential information is findable. Built for handoff. Case study.

  3. Jan 2025 to May 2025

    Web development lead, Indiana Phenology

    Indiana Phenology is a nonprofit for citizen science on seasonal change in nature. A public science resource. Navigation for public visitors and for research users. Case study.

  4. Aug 2025 to Dec 2025

    Web designer, Greater Bloomington Chamber of Commerce

    The Greater Bloomington Chamber of Commerce supports local businesses in Bloomington. Pages with staff, for organisations without a developer on call. Navigation, structure, accessibility. Stakeholder asks became page decisions. Case study.

  5. Aug 2026 to present

    First year as team lead. Accessibility auditor, Town of Nashville

    The Town of Nashville site is the municipal site for Nashville, Brown County, Indiana: council, utilities, police, and town services. Assessment of townofnashville.org. Recommendations only. No live-site edits from the student team. Keyboard and screen-reader paths, then a prioritised list the town can act on after the clinic leaves. Case study. Semester rubric.
