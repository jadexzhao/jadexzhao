About

Same instinct, several rooms

Informatics at Indiana University Luddy, May 2027. Minors in Business Technology and UX (user experience) Research. Hudson & Holland Scholar. First-generation college student. I mentor first-generation and low-income students through FASE MAP (Faculty and Staff for Student Excellence · Mentor Apprenticeship Programme) as Peer Mentor and MAP Coordinator.
