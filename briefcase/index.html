About

Shipped proof, one ladder

Informatics at Luddy is people, information, and technology in one system: who it is for, whether they can use it, whether the organisation can keep it. The Serve IT Clinic is the capstone where that ships: INFO I389, then INFO I489, real partners, staff handoff. Below is what I led, built, and audited. Partner depth lives on the case studies page and the about page.

The clinic

Laurie Burns McRobbie Serve IT Clinic

Who it is for: nonprofits, chambers, and municipal sites whose staff must run the product after students leave. What shipped: accessible navigation, WordPress rebuilds, stakeholder pages, and audit reports. How judged: can they keep using it without a student team on retainer?

The Laurie Burns McRobbie Serve IT Clinic is Luddy's undergraduate tech clinic and Informatics capstone: course credit, a real partner, a handoff staff can keep. INFO I389, then INFO I489: 6 credits over two consecutive semesters, minimum 3 semesters in the clinic.

I joined as a freshman intern, Jan 2024 to May 2024: 19 students from 125 applications (about 6.5% that semester only), starting as accessibility tester for the City of Bloomington. One ladder below, not five jobs.

  1. Jan 2024 to May 2024

    Accessibility tester, City of Bloomington

    Freshman-year intern: WCAG (Web Content Accessibility Guidelines) 2.1 runs, keyboard and screen reader, then written recommendations for navigation and public information. City of Bloomington, IN · Laurie Burns McRobbie Serve IT Clinic; bottom of the ladder, not a second Nashville. City services online have to work for keyboard and screen-reader users after students leave.

    Case study

  2. Aug 2024 to Dec 2024

    WordPress redesign lead, Middle Way House

    WordPress redesign lead with staff and a student team; rebuilt journeys so essential information is findable. Middle Way House is a Bloomington nonprofit supporting people affected by domestic violence, sexual assault, and human trafficking. On a bad night, buried help fails; staff must run the site in January.

    Case study

  3. Jan 2025 to May 2025

    Web development lead, Indiana Phenology

    Web development lead; one navigation structure for public visitors and research users, accessibility inside the layout. Indiana Phenology is a nonprofit for citizen science on seasonal change in Indiana nature. Name which public you mean, then give each arrival a path.

    Case study

  4. Aug 2025 to Dec 2025

    Web designer, Greater Bloomington Chamber of Commerce

    Web designer with staff; stakeholder asks became navigation, structure, and accessible pages. The Greater Bloomington Chamber of Commerce supports local businesses in Bloomington, many without a developer on call. A meeting request is not shipped until someone can find the page.

    Case study

  5. Aug 2026 to present

    First year as team lead. Accessibility auditor, Town of Nashville

    Team lead on an accessibility audit of townofnashville.org; keyboard and screen-reader paths, prioritised recommendations only. The Town of Nashville municipal site covers council, utilities, police, and town services in Brown County, Indiana. Leave a list the town can act on after the clinic leaves; no live-site edits from the student team.
