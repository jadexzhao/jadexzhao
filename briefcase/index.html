Handshake content creator

Students and early-career people · work, identity, systems

why it pairs
I already publish on Handshake. That is how I talk to students and early-career people about work, identity, and systems. Same question as Digital Humans: a persona has to feel real, or people bounce. Career centres already say the profile has to be findable. CU Boulder Career Services tells students a public Handshake profile is five times more likely to be found and messaged by employers (CU Boulder Career Services). IU’s Walter Center says make it public so employers can find you (Walter Center, IU, 2026). UW-Madison Career Services still calls Handshake the number one way college students find jobs (UW-Madison Career Services, 2026). Handshake-claimed market facts, not personal metrics: Class of 2026 students averaged 23 applications per full-time job, double the class of 2023 (Handshake Economic Research, 2026). About 1,600+ educational institutions sit on that network (Handshake About, 2026).
