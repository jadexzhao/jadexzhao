featured · ServeIT

nonprofit sites that survive the handoff

ServeIT is IU’s student-run tech clinic. I lead the website team: scope with nonprofit staff, compose pages from IU Rivet and Cascade chunks, ship WCAG 2.2 AA baselines, leave a plain-english handoff.

ServeIT · website team

problem
partners need sites their staff can edit after we leave, and visitors who use assistive tech need pages that actually work ... not a demo that dies when the semester ends.
what i built
partner pages as a chunk composition (text, callout, CTA, accordion), not custom chrome. Rivet components + Cascade WCMS. language markup and touch targets treated as part of the build, not a late polish pass. handoffs written so a non-developer can keep the page alive.
outcome
WCAG-accessible sites with no developer on call. same instinct as a restaurant floor: the system either survives real customers or it doesn’t.
