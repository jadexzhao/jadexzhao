Jade Zhao. Rising senior at Indiana University Luddy (Informatics; minors in Business Technology and UX Research). Digital Humans Project Lead at Luddy. ServeIT Website Team Lead and Nashville ACC Team Lead / Co-lead. Hudson & Holland Scholar. First-generation college student.
Areas of interest

Technical strengths

That instinct shows up in ServeIT delivery ... clear handoffs, honest scope, and work that holds for real users.
