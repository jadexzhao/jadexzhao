Human agency · AI systems · still building

The human still decides

Convenience is not consent.

I'm an Informatics student working at the intersection of Digital Humans, AI, and human-centered business technology. I care about what happens when a system can speak like a person, make a recommendation, or do the first round for us. The interface can reduce the friction. It cannot quietly take the choice.

My work starts with real rooms: restaurant social that has to work Tuesday to Sunday, Serve IT pages staff can keep, short guides for the next person, and student videos that opened a door. Then I bring the harder question to Digital Humans: what should an intelligent system do for us, what should it disclose, and where must the human stay in charge?
