IU Student Life · S.U.C.C.E.E.D.

One room, not four doors

Hudson & Holland Scholars Program (HHSP) is the scholarship that lets me treat campus as one story: clinic ships, Kelley MSIS (Master of Science in Information Systems) exploration, leadership on stage, and the quieter HHSP community nights that keep a first-generation student grounded. I curate that log for hire proof first; the full S.U.C.C.E.E.D. footprint lives on student life.

Different scholars mix the pillars differently, and that is alright. My stack is clinic + MAP + Digital Humans heavy; yours might be lab + ensemble. Programme tiers are guideposts, not a scoreboard.
