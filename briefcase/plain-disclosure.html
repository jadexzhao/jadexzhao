desk tool · privacy clarity

plain disclosurefour questions before Agree

status shipped origin UE UX desk dates Aug 2025 to Dec 2025

Most people do not read the terms. Even when they do, a lot still do not get it. This page forces a blurb to answer four questions before anyone clicks Agree: what they take, why, where it goes, and what happens if you say no.

Published context (not personal metrics): about 55% missed privacy-policy meaning in an IEEE Access 2021 study; 91% / 97% skip terms (Deloitte, 2017); only 20% say providers are very clear (Deloitte Connected Consumer, 2025); 82% abandoned a brand over data use (Thales, 2025). how I got here

I built this after UX work at IUB Undergraduate Education (Aug 2025 to Dec 2025). The point was never a prettier policy. It was whether someone could tell what was being collected, why, where it went, and what happened if they said no.
