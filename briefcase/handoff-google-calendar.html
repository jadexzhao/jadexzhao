Who it is for

Someone who needs to open Google Calendar, create an event, and share a calendar so others can see the schedule.

Handoff means you can do those jobs without the builder sitting there. Do not write the password on this page.

Same stuck moment that turns into a support ticket. Short steps and a video so you do not have to ping the builder.

Steps

  1. Open calendar.google.com and sign in with the Google account that owns the calendar.
  2. To create an event, click Create (or click an empty time slot). Add a title, date, and time, then Save.
  3. Optional: add guests by email if they need an invite.
  4. To share a whole calendar, find it under My calendars on the left. Open the three dots, then Settings and sharing.
  5. Under Shared with, add a person by email and choose what they can see (details, or free/busy only). Send.

Menus move a little. Stay in the same places: Create, the event Save button, My calendars, and Settings and sharing.
