# Serve IT handoff (sample note)

A public process note for retrieval tests. Serve IT is Indiana
University's student technology clinic. This is not a client page.

## The job

Website Team Lead work is intake, scoping, delegation, and delivery.
The unglamorous part is the handoff after the demo: the page has to
survive contact with a partner who does not have a developer on call.

## What this note does not contain

It does not include partner findings, private tickets, or accessibility
audit details. Those stay with the partner.
